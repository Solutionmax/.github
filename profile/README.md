Based in Spain, working with customers in the Netherlands, Belgium and Spain.

## Amfora

**File sharing with a place for everything.**

Send files through a share link, collect uploads from clients and keep your workspace organized. Set passwords, expiry dates and view limits. People sending or receiving files do not need an account.

Run Amfora with Docker, use bundled storage or connect an S3 compatible provider, and make the interface your own with your logo and colours.

[Explore Amfora](https://amfora.solutionmax.net) · [Documentation](https://amfora.solutionmax.net/docs/) · [Source code](https://github.com/Solutionmax/amfora)

## Pharos

**A status page that does the checking for you.**

Monitor HTTP endpoints, TCP ports and scheduled jobs through heartbeat checks. Pharos opens incidents when checks fail and closes them when services recover, keeping customers informed through a public status page and notifications.

Run it on compatible PHP hosting, including cPanel, DirectAdmin and Plesk, or deploy with Docker.

[Explore Pharos](https://pharos.solutionmax.net) · [Documentation](https://pharos.solutionmax.net/docs/) · [Source code](https://github.com/Solutionmax/pharos)

## Portalis

**One gateway to your servers. A clear record of access.**

Bring SSH access into one place with a browser terminal, session recording and an audit trail. Give people access when they need it, with permissions that expire automatically.

Hosted on your own infrastructure. Preparing for launch.

[Explore Portalis and register interest](https://portalis.sh)
